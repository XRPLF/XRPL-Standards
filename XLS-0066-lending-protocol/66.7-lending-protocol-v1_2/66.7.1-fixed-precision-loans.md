# 66.7.1 Fixed-Precision Loans

## 1. Abstract

Under `LendingProtocolV1_2`, a Vault created with `LEVersion` 2 fixes one precision grid, the base unit, for its whole life ([XLS-65.7.1](../../XLS-0065-single-asset-vault/65.7-lending-protocol-v1_2/65.7.1-fixed-precision.md)). This patch applies that grid to every `LoanBroker` attached to the Vault and every `Loan` issued through those brokers. A Loan's scale is the Vault's base exponent. `LoanBroker.DebtTotal` and the Vault's principal and interest records change by exact amounts. Cash moved into the Vault or the first-loss capital is rounded down so the receiving balance stays representable. New Loans and cover deposits are rejected when they would take the Vault or `LoanBroker` out of its Open zone.

## 2. Motivation

In the parent protocol a Loan fixes its scale at origination from the Vault's `AssetsTotal`, while the Vault's precision follows `AssetsTotal` as it grows. Once a Vault grows past a power of ten, it can no longer represent the amounts of a Loan originated earlier, and that Loan's payments, impairment and default fail with `tecPRECISION_LOSS`. A single grid for the Vault, its brokers and its Loans keeps every Loan representable for its whole life.

## 3. Specification

This patch changes the parent [XLS-66](../README.md) sections named below. The terms base exponent $-P$, base unit $10^{-P}$, live exponent, live unit, coarsened, Open-zone limit $T_{\mathrm{dep}}(P)$, `AssetsDeployed` and `YieldUnrealized` are defined in [XLS-65.7.1](../../XLS-0065-single-asset-vault/65.7-lending-protocol-v1_2/65.7.1-fixed-precision.md). All other parent behavior is unchanged.

### 3.1 Scope

The rules in this patch apply to a `LoanBroker` attached to a Vault with `LEVersion` 2, and to every `Loan` issued through it. A Vault with `LEVersion` 2 is also a cash-basis Vault, so [XLS-66.4.1](../66.4-lending-protocol-v1_1/66.4.1-cash-basis-loans.md) applies as well. Brokers and Loans of Vaults with a lower `LEVersion` are unchanged.

### 3.2 Definitions

For a `LoanBroker`, the reference amount is `LoanBroker.CoverAvailable`. For an IOU asset:

- The posterior live exponent of a change `delta` is the live exponent of `CoverAvailable + delta`: the exponent of that sum in the 16-digit representation, but never below the base exponent.
- An **inflow** of `raw` to `CoverAvailable` is credited as `floor(CoverAvailable + raw) - CoverAvailable`, where `floor` rounds down to the posterior live exponent of the sum.
- An **outflow** of `amount` from `CoverAvailable` is `amount` rounded toward zero to the posterior live exponent of `CoverAvailable - amount`.

For an inflow to the Vault, the reference amount is `Vault.AssetsAvailable`, and the credit is `floor(AssetsAvailable + raw) - AssetsAvailable`, rounded down to the posterior live exponent of the sum.

For XRP and MPT assets, amounts are whole drops or units and these roundings do not apply.

### 3.3 Ledger Entry: `LoanBroker`

#### 3.3.1 State Changes

- `DebtTotal` changes by the exact principal of each `LoanSet`, `LoanPay` and default, and is never rounded to the Vault's live exponent.
- `DebtMaximum` is a multiple of the base unit.
- In the minimum-cover checks of `LoanSet`, `LoanPay`, `LoanBrokerCoverWithdraw` and `LoanBrokerCoverClawback`, the minimum cover `DebtTotal × CoverRateMinimum` is rounded up to the base unit. The default liquidation of `LoanManage` (§3.11.2) does not round it to the base unit.

### 3.4 Ledger Entry: `Loan`

#### 3.4.1 Fields

##### 3.4.1.1 `LoanScale`

`LoanScale` is the Vault's base exponent $-P$. `TotalValueOutstanding`, `PrincipalOutstanding`, `ManagementFeeOutstanding` and every fee field are rounded at that scale. `PeriodicPayment` is stored at full precision.

### 3.5 Transaction: `LoanBrokerSet`

#### 3.5.1 Failure Conditions

In parent §3.3.3.2 check 11, a value field that is not a multiple of the base unit fails with `tecPRECISION_LOSS`.

### 3.6 Transaction: `LoanBrokerCoverDeposit`

#### 3.6.1 Failure Conditions

The deposit is an inflow. Let `credit` be `Amount` credited as defined in §3.2. Added to parent §3.5.3.2, before the funds check:

- `fixCleanup3_2_0`: `credit` is zero. (`tecPRECISION_LOSS`)
- The posterior live exponent of `CoverAvailable + credit` is not the base exponent. (`tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED`)
- `CoverAvailable + credit` exceeds the Open-zone limit $T_{\mathrm{dep}}(P)$. (`tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED`)

The funds check uses `credit`.

#### 3.6.2 State Changes

`credit` is transferred from the submitter to the broker _pseudo-account_, and `LoanBroker.CoverAvailable` increases by `credit`.

### 3.7 Transaction: `LoanBrokerCoverWithdraw`

#### 3.7.1 Failure Conditions

The withdrawal is an outflow. Let `amount` be `Amount` rounded as defined in §3.2. If `amount` is zero, the transaction fails with `tecPRECISION_LOSS`; this replaces the `fixCleanup3_2_0` precision check. All later checks use `amount`. There is no capacity check.

#### 3.7.2 State Changes

`LoanBroker.CoverAvailable` decreases by `amount`, and `amount` is transferred.

### 3.8 Transaction: `LoanBrokerCoverClawback`

#### 3.8.1 Failure Conditions

The clawback is an outflow. `ClawAmount` from parent §3.7.4 is rounded as defined in §3.2. If the result is zero, the transaction fails with `tecPRECISION_LOSS`; this replaces the `fixCleanup3_2_0` precision check.

#### 3.8.2 State Changes

`LoanBroker.CoverAvailable` decreases by the rounded `ClawAmount`, and that amount is clawed back.

### 3.9 Transaction: `LoanSet`

#### 3.9.1 Failure Conditions

Added to parent §3.8.5.2:

- After the `LendingProtocolV1_1` phase checks: the Vault is coarsened, that is, the live exponent of `Vault.AssetsTotal` is not the base exponent. (`tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED`)
- The loan scale is the base exponent, so a value field that is not a multiple of the base unit fails with `tecPRECISION_LOSS`.
- After parent checks 15 to 18 and before check 19: `Vault.AssetsTotal + Vault.YieldUnrealized + InterestDue` exceeds the Open-zone limit $T_{\mathrm{dep}}(P)$, where `InterestDue` is the new Loan's interest owed to the Vault. (`tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED`)

#### 3.9.2 State Changes

Parent §3.8.6 step 6 becomes:

6. Update `Vault` object:
   - Decrease `Vault.AssetsAvailable` by `PrincipalRequested`.
   - Increase `Vault.AssetsDeployed` by `PrincipalRequested`.
   - Increase `Vault.YieldUnrealized` by `InterestDue`.
   - Derive `Vault.AssetsTotal` as defined in XLS-65.7.1.

In step 7, `LoanBroker.DebtTotal` increases by exactly `PrincipalRequested`.

### 3.10 Transaction: `LoanPay`

#### 3.10.1 Failure Conditions

Parent §3.11.4.2 check 17 (`tecPRECISION_LOSS` when the Vault credit does not change `Vault.AssetsAvailable`) does not apply to a payment after which `Loan.PaymentRemaining` is `0`.

#### 3.10.2 State Changes

There is no capacity check. Parent §3.11.5 steps 6 and 7, and the amounts transferred, become:

- `credit` is `principalPaid + interestPaid` credited to the Vault as defined in §3.2.
- `Vault.AssetsAvailable` increases by `credit`.
- `Vault.AssetsDeployed` decreases by `principalPaid`.
- `Vault.YieldUnrealized` changes by the change in `Loan.TotalValueOutstanding - Loan.PrincipalOutstanding - Loan.ManagementFeeOutstanding` caused by the payment.
- `Vault.AssetsTotal` is derived as defined in XLS-65.7.1.
- `LoanBroker.DebtTotal` decreases by exactly `principalPaid`.
- If the fee goes to the cover pool, the fee is credited to `LoanBroker.CoverAvailable` as an inflow defined in §3.2, with no capacity check. If the result is zero, the payment still succeeds.
- The Borrower sends `credit` to the Vault _pseudo-account_ and the credited fee to the fee destination. The difference between these amounts and the computed payment is not charged.

If the payment closes the Loan, `credit` is zero, and either the fee is zero or the fee destination is the Borrower, nothing is transferred.

If the Loan was impaired, the unimpairment before the payment decreases `Vault.LossUnrealized` by `Loan.PrincipalOutstanding`.

### 3.11 Transaction: `LoanManage`

#### 3.11.1 Failure Conditions

This replaces parent §3.10.4.2 check 8: `tfLoanImpair` is specified and `Vault.LossUnrealized + Loan.PrincipalOutstanding > Vault.AssetsDeployed`. (`tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED`) As in the parent, the `fixCleanup3_4_0` check 9 runs before it.

#### 3.11.2 State Changes

Parent §3.10.5 becomes:

1. If the `tfLoanDefault` flag is specified:
   - Compute `DefaultCovered = min((LoanBroker.DebtTotal × CoverRateMinimum) × CoverRateLiquidation, Loan.PrincipalOutstanding, LoanBroker.CoverAvailable)`, where both products are rounded up. For XRP and MPT, round `DefaultCovered` toward zero to a whole unit. For an IOU, credit `DefaultCovered` to the Vault as defined in §3.2.
   - `Vault.AssetsAvailable` increases by `DefaultCovered`.
   - `Vault.AssetsDeployed` decreases by `Loan.PrincipalOutstanding`.
   - `Vault.YieldUnrealized` decreases by `Loan.TotalValueOutstanding - Loan.PrincipalOutstanding - Loan.ManagementFeeOutstanding`.
   - If the Loan is impaired, `Vault.LossUnrealized` decreases by `Loan.PrincipalOutstanding`.
   - `Vault.AssetsTotal` is derived as defined in XLS-65.7.1. The uncovered principal leaves the Vault through this derivation.
   - `LoanBroker.DebtTotal` decreases by exactly `Loan.PrincipalOutstanding`.
   - `LoanBroker.CoverAvailable` decreases by `DefaultCovered`.
   - The `Loan` updates of the parent apply.
   - If `DefaultCovered` is not zero, it is transferred from the `LoanBroker` _pseudo-account_ to the `Vault` _pseudo-account_.
2. If the `tfLoanImpair` flag is specified, `Vault.LossUnrealized` increases by `Loan.PrincipalOutstanding`. The `Loan` updates of the parent apply, including setting `lsfLoanImpaired`.
3. If the `tfLoanUnimpair` flag is specified, `Vault.LossUnrealized` decreases by `Loan.PrincipalOutstanding`. The `Loan` updates of the parent apply, including clearing `lsfLoanImpaired`.

## 4. Rationale

A Loan's schedule is computed at the Vault's base exponent, which never changes, so every Loan stays representable while the Vault grows. Principal and interest records change by exact amounts and never accumulate rounding residue. Cash credited to the Vault or the cover pool is rounded down so that the stored balance equals what the receiving account can hold; the sub-unit remainder is forgiven, never created.

New Loans and cover deposits are optional inflows and are bounded by the Open zone. Payments and fees are owed value and are always admitted, so a Loan can always be repaid. A Loan's scheduled interest is counted against capacity when the Loan is created, so its scheduled payments do not consume capacity later.

A payment that closes a Loan must succeed even if the Vault cannot represent the last sub-unit amount; otherwise the Loan could never be closed.

## 5. Security Considerations

The Vault forgives at most one live unit per credited payment or default. A Loan always closes on its last scheduled payment, and a default always closes the Loan, even when the credited amount is zero.

Implementations must apply the capacity checks only to new Loans and cover deposits. A capacity check on a payment or a redirected fee would let a Vault near capacity block repayment.
