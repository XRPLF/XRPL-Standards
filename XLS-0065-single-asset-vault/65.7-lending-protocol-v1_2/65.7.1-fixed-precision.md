# 65.7.1 Fixed Precision Vault

## 1. Abstract

Each Vault created under this amendment fixes one precision grid for its whole lifetime. The Vault's `Scale` field, $P$, sets the grid at $10^{-P}$. The Lending Protocol side of the amendment issues every Loan through such a Vault at the same grid ([XLS-66.7.1](../../XLS-0066-lending-protocol/66.7-lending-protocol-v1_2/66.7.1-fixed-precision-loans.md)). A Loan's schedule therefore stays representable for its whole life, however large the Vault grows.

A fixed grid gives a fixed capacity. The largest amount representable at $10^{-P}$ with 16 significant digits is $(10^{16}-1) \times 10^{-P}$. Deposits and new Loans are admitted only while the Vault stays within the first 90% of that capacity, the Open zone. The remainder is kept for value the Vault has already earned.

Two Vault fields are added. `AssetsDeployed` records the principal the Vault has lent out. `YieldUnrealized` records the scheduled interest that active Loans owe to the Vault. `AssetsTotal` becomes a derived value: after every operation it is `AssetsAvailable + AssetsDeployed`, rounded down to the asset's 16-digit precision. It carries no accumulated rounding. Capacity is measured as `AssetsTotal + YieldUnrealized`, so a Loan's scheduled interest is reserved against capacity when the Loan is originated.

Vaults created before the amendment are unaffected, apart from the `VaultSet` `AssetsMaximum` check of §3.5.

## 2. Motivation

The Single Asset Vault stores its asset balances as IOU amounts with 16 significant digits. The precision of those balances follows the magnitude of `AssetsTotal`. A Vault holding a small balance carries many decimal places. As deposits raise `AssetsTotal` past each power of ten, the Vault loses one decimal place.

Loans issued through a LoanBroker fix their own precision at origination. A Loan originated while the Vault carried eight decimal places records its schedule at eight decimal places. A later deposit can move the Vault to six decimal places. Every repayment, impairment, and default of that Loan then produces amounts the Vault can no longer represent. The protocol rejects those transactions to protect ledger consistency, and the Loan stays open until `AssetsTotal` falls back below the threshold. The borrower has no action available to close it.

Interest earned by the Vault adds to `AssetsTotal` without any deposit. When `AssetsTotal` sits at the edge of its representable range, that growth has nowhere to go, and a mandatory repayment can fail for the same reason a deposit does.

## 3. Specification

### 3.1. Overview

All behaviour in this specification applies to a Vault whose `LEVersion` is `2` (`FixedPrecision`). `VaultCreate` writes `LEVersion = 2` when `LendingProtocolV1_1`, `LendingProtocolV1_2` and `fixCleanup3_4_0` are all enabled ([XLS-65.4.3](../65.4-lending-protocol-v1_1/65.4.3-cash-basis-vault.md)). A `FixedPrecision` Vault keeps cash-basis interest recognition. The one rule that applies to every Vault once `LendingProtocolV1_2` is enabled is the `VaultSet` check of §3.5.

Throughout, an **optional inflow** is value added at the submitter's discretion: `VaultDeposit`, and a new Loan in `LoanSet`. **Mandatory growth** is value the Vault has already earned: interest and principal received through `LoanPay`, and First-Loss Capital received on default. An **outflow** removes cash from the Vault: `VaultWithdraw` and `VaultClawback`.

### 3.2. Arithmetic

#### 3.2.1. Representation

A non-zero IOU amount has the form $m \times 10^{e}$ with $10^{15} \le m \le 10^{16}-1$. An IOU amount carries 16 significant digits. Write $e_{\mathrm{dyn}}(T)$ for the exponent of amount $T$ in this form, rounding $T$ down to 16 digits. When $T$ grows past $(10^{16}-1) \times 10^{e}$, the exponent increases by one and the smallest representable step grows tenfold. When $T$ shrinks below $10^{15} \times 10^{e}$, the exponent decreases by one.

XRP amounts are integers of drops. MPT amounts are integers of MPT units. Both have exponent 0.

#### 3.2.2. Scale

**Scale** $P$ is an integer with $0 \le P \le 10$, stored on the Vault at creation and fixed for the life of the Vault. The **base exponent** is $-P$ and the **base unit** is $10^{-P}$. For XRP and MPT, $P = 0$.

#### 3.2.3. Live exponent

The **live exponent** of a reference amount $R$ is

$$
e(R) = \max\left(-P,\; e_{\mathrm{dyn}}(R)\right), \qquad e(0) = -P .
$$

The **live unit** is $10^{e(R)}$ and the **live scale** is $s = -e(R)$, the number of decimal places of the live unit. While $R$ fits at the base exponent, the live exponent equals the base exponent. When $R$ grows beyond $(10^{16}-1) \times 10^{-P}$, the live exponent **coarsens**. When $R$ shrinks back, it re-fines, down to the base exponent.

The Vault is **coarsened** when the live exponent of `AssetsTotal` is above $-P$.

#### 3.2.4. Operation rule

An operation that moves cash into or out of the Vault changes `AssetsAvailable` by a delta $\Delta$. The delta is rounded before it is applied:

| Operation                                       | Reference amount $R$ | Rounding of $\Delta$                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `VaultDeposit`                                  | `AssetsTotal`        | Toward zero at $e(R + \Delta)$, where $R + \Delta$ is evaluated with round-to-nearest.                                   |
| `VaultWithdraw`, `VaultClawback`                | `AssetsAvailable`    | Magnitude toward zero at $e(R - \lvert\Delta\rvert)$, where $R - \lvert\Delta\rvert$ is evaluated with round-to-nearest. |
| `LoanPay` credit, First-Loss Capital on default | `AssetsAvailable`    | $R + \Delta$ is rounded down at its own live exponent; the applied delta is that result minus $R$.                       |

The outflow grid is the posterior grid of `AssetsAvailable`, the balance the cash actually leaves. It is not the grid of the derived `AssetsTotal`.

A rounded delta of zero fails the transaction with `tecPRECISION_LOSS` for:

- `VaultDeposit`;
- `VaultWithdraw` and `VaultClawback`, except where the parent already allows shares to be redeemed or burned for zero assets: a withdrawal that redeems every outstanding share, a share-denominated withdrawal from a Vault whose `AssetsTotal` equals its `LossUnrealized`, and a `VaultClawback` by the Vault Owner that burns a holder's shares;
- a `LoanPay` after which the Loan's `PaymentRemaining` is not zero.

A withdrawal or clawback whose non-zero rounded delta leaves the stored `AssetsAvailable` unchanged also fails with `tecPRECISION_LOSS`.

Two Vault credits may round to zero and still succeed, with no cash moved: a `LoanPay` that closes the Loan (its `PaymentRemaining` becomes zero), and the First-Loss Capital of a `LoanManage` default. In both cases the Loan's accounting still changes, and the Vault forgives the sub-unit remainder ([XLS-66.7.1](../../XLS-0066-lending-protocol/66.7-lending-protocol-v1_2/66.7.1-fixed-precision-loans.md)).

For XRP and MPT the rounding is a no-op.

#### 3.2.5. Capacity and zones

The Vault's capacity value is

$$
C_{\mathrm{vault}} = \mathrm{AssetsTotal} + \mathrm{YieldUnrealized},
$$

evaluated on the posterior state with the delta rounded toward zero. The Open-zone limit is

$$
T_{\mathrm{dep}}(P) = 9 \times 10^{15} \times 10^{-P},
$$

which is 90% of the largest amount representable at the base exponent.

| Zone   | Posterior capacity                           | Optional inflows              | Mandatory growth                |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Open   | $C_{\mathrm{vault}} \le T_{\mathrm{dep}}(P)$ | Admitted                      | Admitted                        |
| Beyond | $C_{\mathrm{vault}} > T_{\mathrm{dep}}(P)$   | Fail with `tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED` | Admitted; the Vault may coarsen |

An optional inflow is also admitted only when the posterior live exponent of `AssetsTotal` equals the base exponent. A coarsened Vault rejects optional inflows with `tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED` until it re-fines.

Only optional inflows are checked against the zones, each on its own posterior state: `VaultDeposit` (§3.7) and `LoanSet` (§3.10). Mandatory growth and outflows are never checked. A Vault whose capacity falls back into the Open zone admits optional inflows again.

#### 3.2.6. Vault balances

**`AssetsDeployed`** is the principal the Vault has lent out and not yet received back.

**`YieldUnrealized`** is the scheduled interest that active Loans owe to the Vault and that it has yet to receive. Each Lending Protocol transaction changes it by the change in the affected Loan's `TotalValueOutstanding - PrincipalOutstanding - ManagementFeeOutstanding`. The transactions that maintain it are specified in [XLS-66.7.1](../../XLS-0066-lending-protocol/66.7-lending-protocol-v1_2/66.7.1-fixed-precision-loans.md).

`YieldUnrealized` takes no part in share conversions. Share price is computed from `AssetsTotal` and `LossUnrealized` as before.

**`AssetsTotal` is derived.** Every read of the Vault's total value, including share conversions, uses `AssetsAvailable + AssetsDeployed`. After every operation the stored `AssetsTotal` is set to that sum, rounded down to the asset's 16-digit precision. While the sum fits in 16 digits it is exact. Above that, the stored value is a floor of the exact sum, recomputed on every operation rather than carried forward. Because `AssetsDeployed` is never negative, the stored `AssetsTotal` is never below `AssetsAvailable`.

`AssetsAvailable` is the Vault pseudo-account's cash. It changes by the rounded delta of §3.2.4 and is stored at the asset's 16-digit precision. After coarsening it can keep a residue finer than the live unit.

`AssetsDeployed`, `LossUnrealized` and `YieldUnrealized` move no cash and are changed by exact amounts taken from the Loan. Each must remain exactly representable at the asset's 16-digit precision.

All five fields are written by a single balance update. That update fails the transaction with `tefBAD_LEDGER` if any of `AssetsAvailable`, `AssetsDeployed`, `LossUnrealized` or `YieldUnrealized` would become negative. It fails with `tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED` if `LossUnrealized` would exceed `AssetsDeployed`.

`AssetsMaximum` is compared against the derived `AssetsTotal` alone.

### 3.3. Ledger Entry: `Vault`

#### 3.3.1. Fields

The Vault ledger entry gains two fields, and the meaning of two existing fields changes. All other fields are unchanged.

| Field Name        | Constant | Required | JSON Type | Internal Type | Default Value | Description                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------- | :------: | :------: | :-------: | :-----------: | :-----------: | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `AssetsDeployed`  |    No    |    No    | `string`  |   `NUMBER`    |      `0`      | Principal lent out by the Vault and not yet received back. Zero at creation.                                                      |
| `YieldUnrealized` |    No    |    No    | `string`  |   `NUMBER`    |      `0`      | Scheduled interest owed to the Vault by active Loans and not yet received. Zero at creation.                                      |
| `Scale`           |   Yes    |    No    | `number`  |    `UINT8`    |      `6`      | The scale $P$. Range 0 to 10. Default 6 for an IOU asset. Absent for XRP and MPT, which means 0. Fixed for the life of the Vault. |
| `LEVersion`       |   Yes    |    No    | `number`  |    `UINT8`    |      `2`      | `2` (`FixedPrecision`) for Vaults created under this amendment.                                                                   |

#### 3.3.2. Invariants

For a Vault with `LEVersion` 2, these hold after every transaction:

1. `AssetsTotal` equals `AssetsAvailable + AssetsDeployed`, rounded down to the asset's 16-digit precision.
2. `AssetsDeployed >= 0`, and it is exactly representable at the asset's 16-digit precision.
3. `YieldUnrealized >= 0`.
4. `LossUnrealized <= AssetsDeployed`. This replaces the comparison of `LossUnrealized` with `AssetsTotal - AssetsAvailable` in parent [§3.1.10](../README.md#3110-invariants) invariant 5.
5. If the share `OutstandingAmount` is zero, `AssetsDeployed` is zero, in addition to parent invariant 7.
6. A newly created Vault has `AssetsDeployed` and `YieldUnrealized` equal to zero.
7. `AssetsDeployed` and `YieldUnrealized` change only in `LoanSet`, `LoanPay` and `LoanManage`. `LossUnrealized` changes only in `LoanManage` and `LoanPay`, as in parent invariant 15.

The parent accounting-delta invariants of `VaultDeposit`, `VaultWithdraw` and `VaultClawback` change as follows:

- The change in `AssetsAvailable` must equal the exact change in the Vault pseudo-account's balance, with no rounding tolerance.
- The change in `AssetsTotal` is not compared with the pseudo-account's balance change, because `AssetsTotal` is a derived floor (invariant 1 covers it).
- The sign checks on the Vault's balance change use the exact change, not the change rounded to the `AssetsTotal` scale.

### 3.4. Transaction: `VaultCreate`

#### 3.4.1. Failure Conditions

##### 3.4.1.1. Data Verification

- `Scale` is present and greater than 10. (`temMALFORMED`) This replaces the limit of 18 for a Vault that will be created with `LEVersion` 2.

##### 3.4.1.2. Protocol-Level Failures

- `AssetsMaximum` is present and is not a multiple of $10^{-P}$ that is exactly representable for the asset. (`tecPRECISION_LOSS`) $P$ is `Scale`, or 6 when `Scale` is absent and the asset is an IOU.

#### 3.4.2. State Changes

On success the Vault records `LEVersion = 2`, `AssetsDeployed = 0` and `YieldUnrealized = 0`.

### 3.5. Transaction: `VaultSet`

#### 3.5.1. Failure Conditions

This rule applies to every Vault once `LendingProtocolV1_2` is enabled, whatever its `LEVersion`:

- `AssetsMaximum` is present and is not exactly representable at the Vault's scale. (`tecPRECISION_LOSS`) For a Vault with `LEVersion` 2 the scale is the base exponent $-P$. For other Vaults it is the current exponent of `AssetsTotal`.

`Scale` cannot be changed.

### 3.6. Transaction: `VaultDelete`

Unchanged. The parent check that `AssetsTotal` is zero reads the derived total, so a Vault with non-zero `AssetsDeployed` cannot be deleted.

### 3.7. Transaction: `VaultDeposit`

#### 3.7.1. Failure Conditions

##### 3.7.1.1. Protocol-Level Failures

`VaultDeposit` is an optional inflow. The deposit is rounded as in §3.2.4 and the shares are computed from it. Then, before the `AssetsMaximum` check of the parent:

1. The posterior live exponent of `AssetsTotal` is not the base exponent $-P$. (`tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED`)
2. The posterior capacity `AssetsTotal + YieldUnrealized` exceeds $T_{\mathrm{dep}}(P)$. (`tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED`)

For XRP and MPT the minimum deposit is one drop or one MPT unit.

### 3.8. Transaction: `VaultWithdraw`

VaultWithdraw is an outflow and has no zone check. The payout is rounded as in §3.2.4. The Vault may re-fine as a result.

#### 3.8.1. Failure Conditions

##### 3.8.1.1. Protocol-Level Failures

- The withdrawal redeems every outstanding share while `AssetsDeployed` is non-zero. (`tecHAS_OBLIGATIONS`) The shares still have a claim on principal that is out on loan.

#### 3.8.2. State Changes

A withdrawal that redeems every outstanding share pays out `AssetsAvailable` ([XLS-65.2](../65.2-fix-cleanup-3_2_0/README.md)). It applies that payout through the balance update of §3.2.6, so it does not set `AssetsTotal` and `AssetsAvailable` to zero directly.

### 3.9. Transaction: `VaultClawback`

VaultClawback is an outflow and has no zone check. The recovered amount is rounded as in §3.2.4. The Vault may re-fine as a result.

#### 3.9.1. Failure Conditions

##### 3.9.1.1. Protocol-Level Failures

- The clawback would burn every outstanding share while `AssetsDeployed` is non-zero. (`tecHAS_OBLIGATIONS`)

### 3.10. Lending Protocol transactions

The Lending Protocol changes the Vault only through the balance update of §3.2.6. The amounts are computed by [XLS-66.7.1](../../XLS-0066-lending-protocol/66.7-lending-protocol-v1_2/66.7.1-fixed-precision-loans.md). The effect on the Vault is:

| Transaction           | `AssetsAvailable`                                   | `AssetsDeployed`         | `YieldUnrealized`                         | `LossUnrealized`                                                                  |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `LoanSet`             | − principal                                         | + principal              | + the Loan's interest due                 | unchanged                                                                         |
| `LoanPay`             | + principal and interest paid, rounded as in §3.2.4 | − principal paid         | + change in the Loan's scheduled interest | − `PrincipalOutstanding` if the Loan was impaired, which the payment clears first |
| `LoanManage` impair   | unchanged                                           | unchanged                | unchanged                                 | + `PrincipalOutstanding`                                                          |
| `LoanManage` unimpair | unchanged                                           | unchanged                | unchanged                                 | − `PrincipalOutstanding`                                                          |
| `LoanManage` default  | + First-Loss Capital applied, rounded as in §3.2.4  | − `PrincipalOutstanding` | − the Loan's remaining scheduled interest | − `PrincipalOutstanding` if impaired                                              |

`LoanSet` is an optional inflow. It fails with `tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED` when the Vault is coarsened, or when `AssetsTotal + YieldUnrealized` plus the new Loan's interest due exceeds $T_{\mathrm{dep}}(P)$. An impairment that would make `LossUnrealized` exceed `AssetsDeployed` fails with `tecLIMIT_EXCEEDED`.

### 3.11. Legacy objects

A Vault with `LEVersion` below 2 keeps the behaviour it was created with. Its precision follows `AssetsTotal`, `AssetsTotal` is stored rather than derived, and the existing reconciliation tolerances apply. The capacity zones, `AssetsDeployed`, `YieldUnrealized` and the fixed grid apply only to Vaults with `LEVersion` 2. The `VaultSet` check of §3.5 is the exception.

## 4. Rationale

A fixed grid gives a fixed capacity. Dividing it into an Open zone for optional inflows and headroom for mandatory growth means scheduled repayments never fail for lack of precision. A Loan is admitted only when the whole of its scheduled interest fits in the Open zone alongside the current balance. When that interest is later paid it moves from `YieldUnrealized` into `AssetsTotal`, so capacity is unchanged.

`AssetsTotal` is derived rather than accumulated. Under cash-basis accounting it equals the Vault's cash plus the principal it has lent. Storing the principal as `AssetsDeployed` and recomputing the total after every operation removes every rounding residue an incremented total would carry once the Vault has coarsened. It also lets impairment, default and full redemption read principal exactly, instead of inferring it from a rounded difference.

Rounding `AssetsTotal` down rather than to nearest means the derived total never overstates the assets behind the shares.

Outflows round at the posterior grid of `AssetsAvailable` because that is the balance the cash leaves. Rounding at the coarser `AssetsTotal` grid would discard digits that the cash balance can still hold.

`YieldUnrealized` takes no part in share conversions. Share price is computed from `AssetsTotal` and `LossUnrealized` as before.

## 5. Security Considerations

An optional inflow is admitted only when the Vault is at its base exponent and stays within the Open zone, so a depositor cannot push the Vault into a state where scheduled repayments lose precision.

A `VaultDeposit`, `VaultWithdraw` or `VaultClawback` whose rounded delta is zero fails with `tecPRECISION_LOSS`, so shares are never minted or burned for no cash, except in the zero-value cases of §3.2.4 that the parent already allows. A closing `LoanPay` or a default may credit the Vault zero. The forgiven amount is below one live unit, and it lets the Loan close.

Because `AssetsTotal` is recomputed from exact components after every operation, no rounding residue survives. A full redemption or clawback of every share is refused while `AssetsDeployed` is non-zero. Otherwise a Vault could be left with no shares but principal still on loan.

`YieldUnrealized`, `AssetsDeployed` and `LossUnrealized` are never allowed to go negative, and `LossUnrealized` cannot exceed `AssetsDeployed`. A Loan can only be impaired for principal the Vault actually has on loan.

Vaults created before the amendment keep the behaviour they were created with. The `VaultSet` `AssetsMaximum` check applies to them too, but it only rejects a cap that would otherwise be rounded silently.
